# 30DayMapChallenge 2026 — NG Cartography

## Day 08 · Utopia

**Vladivostok, Noon.** An imagined 22nd-century archipelago of shared learning,
creative work and stays, inspired by Arkady and Boris Strugatsky. Three registered
layers distinguish real surface relief, recorded built places with proposed
shared uses, and recorded ways with imagined cooperation. Future access and
programmes are scenarios, not operating services or a development plan.

The approved PNG is unchanged, 1800 × 2600. A JPEG preview, layered SVG with
outlined typography and derived geographic data under ODbL accompany the entry.
The SVG includes raster terrain and dense fabric plus vector overlays; no font
software is embedded. Source mapping: © OpenStreetMap contributors, 25 October
2026; terrain: Copernicus GLO-30 Public 2021, height ×3. No raw terrain tiles,
reference images or rejected studies are published. Full sources and limits
are on the gallery page.


## Day 07 · 10 minute map

**City of stairs / Vladivostok.** An after-timer edition linking a grouped
mainland overview to a one-kilometre central detail. OpenStreetMap stairway line
records share compact overview dots; open transverse strokes identify individual
segments in the detail. The overview contains 8,029 selected records in 1,003
display groups; neither number counts separate staircases. Short detailed symbols
are enlarged for reading. Mapping completeness and access are not verified.

The unchanged timed stairway study stopped after 246.8 seconds on 22 November
2026 and has separate PNG and SVG downloads. The overview-and-detail sheet was
developed afterwards and is explicitly marked as an after-timer edition.
PNG 3500 × 2250, editable all-vector SVG with embedded open fonts, JPEG preview,
and derived GeoJSON data under ODbL are linked from the gallery. Source:
© OpenStreetMap contributors, local snapshot 25 October 2026. Font licences
remain in the SVG metadata. The accepted map image is published without changes.


## Day 06 · Vintage

**Vladivostok / By steamship, 1881–1907.** Selected regular sea connections in
six colour families, linked to ten dated chronology events. Calls and dates
follow the Japan Center for Asian Historical Records route diagrams and
chronology. Curves connect recorded calls schematically; they are not ship
tracks or a complete network operating in one year. The coastline is modern
Natural Earth 1:10 million; locations are representative city or island-group
coordinates from the Gazetteer of Japan (2021), GeoNames and the United States
National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. English PNG 2560 × 3360, all-vector SVG
with outlined text and a JPEG preview. Sources, method and limits accompany
the gallery entry. No generated art study is included in the released map.

## Day 05 · Senses: Sight

**Sea visibility / Vladivostok.** Overview visibility of a sea cell within five
kilometres, from observers 1.7 metres above a digital surface. The surface includes
vegetation and structures. Positions are spaced 150 metres apart; the 30 metre
surface is sampled in 72 directions. Street-level views are not verified.

The English map combines source-based cartography with a generated decorative
paper-and-sea background. Hatching on land encodes model results; pale water
textures and arcs are decorative. PNG 3360 × 4830, layered SVG with outlined
typography, and a JPEG preview are provided. Method, limits, source dates and
attribution accompany the gallery entry. Sources: Copernicus GLO-30 Public 2021
and OpenStreetMap snapshot 25 October 2026. No raw datasets are redistributed.

## Day 04 · Clusters

**Where typhoons go / Two route families, one Pacific.** One common map of 320
western Pacific cyclone life histories from 2000–2024: 112 westward, 190 turning
north, and 18 borderline assignments. Measured passage fields summarise each
family; two real medoids and all 18 borderline routes remain individual lines.
Dashed segments distinguish extratropical continuations. Fields and proximity
are not hazard or forecast probabilities. Data: NOAA IBTrACS v04r01, WP, accessed
6 November 2026; dataset DOI 10.25921/82ty-9e16, Knapp et al. (2010),
DOI 10.1175/2009BAMS2755.1; Natural Earth; OpenStreetMap contributors / ODbL.
English PNG 3780 × 4830, editable SVG and web preview; full method, regional
counts, limitations and credits accompany the map on the gallery page.

## Day 03 · Polygons

**Vladivostok / Between hills & bays.** Generalised building patterns across
the peninsula and islands, with flat surface-elevation bands and three
equal-scale details: central Vladivostok, Vtoraya Rechka and Patrokl Bay.
Overture 2026-10-21.0 / OpenStreetMap / Microsoft ML Buildings; OSM coastline
snapshot 2026-10-25; Copernicus GLO-30 release 2021. PNG, editable SVG and
derived fabric GeoJSON (ODbL) are linked from the gallery. Study window is
not the complete administrative city boundary; generalisation is not a
measurement of building area. Full method and attribution accompany the map.

A public cartographic notebook, published with GitHub Pages.

## Day 01 · Points

**Primorye — Settlements & Terrain.** 657 settlements in Primorsky Krai, shown
against a digital hachure drawing of the terrain. Symbol sizes use population
figures for **2020**, from the CPUR release dated 12 March 2021.

The map is an exploration of settlement patterns and landforms, not a causal model.
Twelve zero values are retained and do not establish abandonment.

The page provides a web preview, the full-resolution PNG, and an editable SVG.
The workflow uses Python and AI-assisted scripting, with iterative cartographic
review and refinement.

## Site structure

This is a buildless static site: `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `assets/`.
GitHub Pages publishes the `main` branch from the repository root. `.nojekyll`
keeps the files as a plain static website. No package installation is required.

Add future challenge days as new map entries and keep their credits, dates,
licences, and image descriptions with the published work.

## Day 02 · Lines

**Bikin / Hydrographic herbarium.** HydroRIVERS network and 88 State Water
Register tributary records: 51 left-bank, 37 right-bank, 16 unnamed. Distances
follow the register; branch lengths are schematic. HydroRIVERS source grid:
15 arc-seconds, junction-preserving smoothing. Lehner & Grill (2013),
doi:10.1002/hyp.9740. Five name links through OpenStreetMap, 25 October 2026.
Sources, method and limitations accompany the gallery map. English PNG and
editable SVG, 3360 × 3990 pixels.
